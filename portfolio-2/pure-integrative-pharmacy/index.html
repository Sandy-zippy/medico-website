This being our team’s second project with Pure Pharmacy, we were familiar with the need for a functional design that maintains a sense of modernity.

Plan a clinic like this More pharmacies

With this build, our key focus was on building task-specific areas, not only does this allow for an increase in productivity, but it also maximizes product placement for pharmacy shelving within the retail area. Elements such as specialized lighting and millwork are seen throughout the displays, fixtures, and the reception area which were strategically placed to help facilitate patient flow and deliver a better customer experience overall. Finally, to ensure safety, custom security shutters for the dispensary and compounding were wall-integrated for added protection.
