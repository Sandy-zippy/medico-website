# Compounding pharmacy design build

*By Dal Riarh, President and CEO, The Medico Group. Last updated 27 November 2026.*

We plan, design and build compounding pharmacies and central compounding labs under one contract, from the site check to certified rooms. One team owns the clean rooms, the non-sterile rooms and the building around them, so the air system, structure and College paperwork are priced and planned together from the start. We work in BC and Alberta.

## What does compounding pharmacy design build include?

**Site check, room program, design and engineering, College drawings, construction, commissioning, and coordination with your certifier, all under one contract and one budget.**

A compounding lab is a facility in its own right. It can hold sterile clean rooms for hazardous and non-hazardous work, non-sterile compounding rooms, gowning and anterooms, storage, shipping, offices and staff space. Each room has its own air, pressure and finish rules. In design-build, we bring the designer and engineers in under our contract, price as the drawings develop, and build what we priced. Our [design-build vs design-bid-build guide](/guides/design-build-vs-design-bid-build/) explains the difference.

## Who is it for?

**Owners of private compounding pharmacies and central compounding facilities, and pharmacies adding a sterile suite, a hazardous room or more compounding space.**

Typical clients include:

- a pharmacy moving compounding out of a retail store into a dedicated lab in a light-industrial unit
- a growing compounder expanding into the unit next door
- a pharmacy adding a NAPRA sterile suite or a hazardous non-sterile room
- a pharmacy and clinic sharing one fit-out

Hospital and health authority teams buy this work differently, through prequalification and tenders. See our [hospital pharmacy NAPRA upgrade](/pharmacy-clean-rooms/hospital-pharmacy/) page.

## What should you check before you sign a lease?

**Slab, roof, structure, electrical service, exhaust routes and strata or landlord rules. Each one can add months or a large change order if found late.**

| Check | Why it matters | What we saw on our projects |
|---|---|---|
| Slab | Modular pods need a flat, strong slab; drains need trenching | At our [Coastal Care compounding lab](/pharmacy-clean-rooms/projects/coastal-care-compounding-lab/), the slab was trenched and re-poured |
| Structure | Air handlers are heavy and often go up high | The same lab needed a reinforced mezzanine to carry the air handler |
| Roof | Condensers, exhaust fans and stacks need curbs and penetrations | On a new building under roof warranty, plan for the original roofer |
| Electrical service | Air handlers, fans and humidifiers add load | The lab needed a service upgrade and a new transformer |
| Exhaust and fresh air | Hazardous rooms exhaust outdoors; fresh air needs louvres | Route exhaust before you lock the room layout |
| Strata or landlord | Business parks often review drawings before tender | Add a review step to the schedule |
| Zoning and occupancy | Light-industrial units can bring different code rules | Confirm with the city and your code consultant |

Our [pharmacy design guide](/pharmacy/guides/design/) covers the College's dispensary rules if your lab also dispenses.

## How does a project run?

**Program, site check, design with live pricing, College notice, permit, construction, commissioning and certification, then College evidence and a full handover pack.**

| Step | What happens | Who leads |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Program | Rooms, ISO classes, pressures, equipment, workflow, growth | You and us |
| 2. Site check | Slab, roof, structure, service, exhaust, landlord rules | Us, with engineers |
| 3. Design and budget | Drawings and mechanical design, priced as they develop | Our designer and engineers |
| 4. College notice | In BC, Form 8G and a scaled diagram at least 30 days before work; in Alberta, ACP renovation approval | You, with our drawings |
| 5. Permit and orders | Building permit; long-lead air handlers, doors, pass-throughs or pod | Us |
| 6. Construction | Shell, services, rooms, finishes | Us |
| 7. Commissioning | Balancing, controls, pressure monitoring and alarms | Us and the mechanical trade |
| 8. Certification | Room and C-PEC testing, air and surface sampling | Your certifier, coordinated by us |
| 9. College evidence | Inspection report and photos within 14 days of completion (BC) | You, with our handover pack |

Sources: [CPBC change in layout](https://www.bcpharmacists.org/node/1758), [ACP](https://abpharmacy.ca/news/pharmacy-renovations-require-approval-acp/). For a new BC pharmacy, the College estimates 6 to 12 months to open ([CPBC licensure guide](https://library.bcpharmacists.org/3_Registration_Licensure/5237-Pharmacy-Licensure-Guide.pdf)). See [how long to build a pharmacy clean room](/pharmacy-clean-rooms/guides/cleanroom-project-timeline/).

## Built-in or modular clean rooms?

**Both work. Built-in rooms use framed walls and FRP; modular pods are factory-built panels. Either way, we build the shell, services and HVAC around them.**

At Coastal Care Pharmacy we built the sterile suite in place. At the Coastal Care compounding lab, a factory-built pod goes inside a shell we prepare. A pod supplier's scope usually stops at the pod. On that job the supplier excluded items such as permits, final certification, roof and wall penetrations, exhaust and fresh-air ducts and hoods. Price those on day one. Compare the options on our [modular cleanroom pharmacy](/pharmacy-clean-rooms/modular-vs-built-in/) page.

## What have we built?

**Two Surrey compounding projects: a sterile suite and non-sterile room built in 2020, and a modular compounding lab with sterile and non-sterile rooms in progress.**

- [Coastal Care Pharmacy sterile suite](/pharmacy-clean-rooms/projects/coastal-care-sterile-suite/), 2020: a hazardous sterile suite and a non-sterile room, built as construction manager to an interior designer's drawings.
- [Coastal Care compounding lab](/pharmacy-clean-rooms/projects/coastal-care-compounding-lab/), in progress: about 3,388 sq ft with a modular pod for sterile and non-sterile work, hazardous and non-hazardous. We are general contractor for everything around the pod.

We also built the compounding room at [Humble Compounding](/pharmacy-clean-rooms/projects/humble-compounding/) in Langley in 2026: a negative-pressure non-sterile hazardous compounding room in a combined pharmacy and clinic. That was a construction job from an interior designer's drawings, not a design-build job, and it is not a clean room.

We have also priced a compounding centre expansion of more than 6,000 sq ft with sterile hazardous and non-hazardous rooms for another design team. Our questions on that tender set showed what bid drawings tend to leave out: equipment data, the plumbing fixture schedule, water heater size, ceiling heights and clean room window details. Design-build closes those gaps before pricing, not after.

## FAQ

### Do you design compounding pharmacies or only build them?
Both. In design-build, our designer and engineers work under our contract. We also build from your own designer's drawings, as at Coastal Care Pharmacy.

### Can a compounding lab go in a light-industrial unit?
Yes. The Coastal Care compounding lab we are building is in a light-industrial unit. Check the slab, roof, structure, electrical service and zoning first.

### Do you handle College of Pharmacists paperwork?
The licence holder files it. We provide the scaled drawings, construction dates and handover photos the College asks for.

### Do you work in Alberta?
Yes. We work in BC and Alberta. In Alberta, the Alberta College of Pharmacy requires approval for pharmacy renovations.

### Who certifies the clean rooms?
An independent certifier tests the rooms and C-PECs. We commission the systems first and coordinate the visit. See [pharmacy cleanroom certification](/pharmacy-clean-rooms/certification-commissioning/).
