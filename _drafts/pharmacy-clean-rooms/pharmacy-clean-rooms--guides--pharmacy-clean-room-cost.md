# Pharmacy clean room cost in Canada: benchmarks and drivers

*By Dal Riarh, President and CEO, The Medico Group. Last updated 27 November 2026.*

A pharmacy clean room costs far more per square foot than the rest of a pharmacy, and the air system is the main reason. Public Canadian hospital tenders for NAPRA upgrades and sterile compounding suites have been listed at about $0.35M to $6.25M, and US sources put compounding clean rooms at US$500 to US$1,200 per sq ft. HVAC is often 35 to 55 percent of the clean room budget.

## What have Canadian hospital clean room projects cost?

**Listed values on public BC tenders run from about $350,000 for a small NAPRA upgrade to $6.25M for a new hazardous and non-hazardous suite.**

Health authority tenders are the best public data on what this work costs in Canada. The values below are as listed on tender sites. Some are estimates, some are award values, and one project was cancelled, so treat them as reference points, not quotes.

|---|---|---|
| Peace Arch Hospital (Fraser Health) | New hazardous and non-hazardous sterile suites, construction management | $6,250,000 |
| qathet General Hospital (VCH) | Three rooms converted to HD and non-HD clean rooms, anteroom and change room (cancelled) | $2,899,000 |
| Nanaimo Regional General Hospital (Island Health) | Pharmacy construction for unit dose and sterile products standards, 2016 | $2,500,000 |
| Royal Jubilee Hospital (Island Health) | CIVA HVAC and pass-through upgrade | $1,475,000 |

That averages about $1.6M per pharmacy.

## What do per square foot benchmarks say?

**US sources put compounding clean rooms at roughly US$500 to US$1,200 per sq ft, with ISO 7 rooms costing more than ISO 8 rooms.**

We have found no published Canadian per square foot figures for pharmacy clean rooms. These US figures are the best public benchmarks. They are in US dollars and for US projects, so use them as a sense check only.

| Source (US) | Figure (USD) |
|---|---|
| [Terrapin CG, 2026](https://terrapincg.com/news/cleanroom-construction-cost-per-square-foot-2026) | USP 797 compounding rooms $500 to $1,200 per sq ft; ISO 7 $400 to $650; ISO 8 $250 to $450 |
| [R&D World](https://www.rdworldonline.com/the-costs-of-building-a-cleanroom/) | ISO 8 to ISO 7 rooms from $250 to $1,500 per sq ft or more |
| [ACCC, USP 800 cost presentation](https://accc-cancer.org/docs/projects/open/presentations/true-cost-of-usp-800-compliance-forrey.pdf) | One hospital buffer room at $1,120 per sq ft all in, against $154 per sq ft for office space |

## Why does HVAC take so much of the budget?

**The rooms need far more air than normal space: 30 air changes an hour, HEPA filters, set pressures, outdoor exhaust and alarmed controls.**

Terrapin puts HVAC and filtration at 35 to 55 percent of a clean room budget. In the ACCC example, one infusion centre's HVAC was budgeted at US$99,400 and came in at US$297,963. Price the air system from mechanical drawings, not from a floor plan.

On a compounding centre expansion of more than 6,000 sq ft with sterile hazardous and non-hazardous rooms that we have priced, HVAC was the largest single trade by a wide margin, inside that 35 to 55 percent range. The NAPRA numbers drive it: see our [NAPRA cleanroom requirements](/pharmacy-clean-rooms/guides/napra-cleanroom-requirements/) guide.

## Why can two per square foot numbers both be right?

**A cost per sq ft over a whole suite, including offices and storage, is much lower than one over the classified rooms alone.**

A compounding facility includes gowning, support, storage, washrooms and offices around the ISO rooms. Spread the clean room cost over all of it and the figure drops. When you compare quotes or benchmarks, ask which area the number covers. We price classified rooms and the wider suite as separate lines so owners can compare like with like.

## What else drives the cost?

**Room count and ISO class, structure and roof work for equipment, electrical service, phasing in a live pharmacy, and doors, pass-throughs and monitoring.**

- **Rooms and airlocks.** Each anteroom, clean room and storage room adds walls, doors, supply and exhaust.
- **Structure and roof.** Peace Arch's scope included structural work for overhead equipment. qathet needed work on courtyard pads and the hospital roof.
- **Electrical service.** Air handlers, exhaust fans and humidifiers can outgrow the existing service.
- **Phasing.** UHNBC's tender started with demolition of the main compounding area and a temporary non-hazardous compounding room, so the pharmacy could stay open.
- **Live hospital rules.** Infection control during construction, and Interior Health makes after-hours overtime "the Contractor's responsibility" ([IH requirements](https://www.interiorhealth.ca/sites/default/files/PDFS/owners-general-requirements.pdf)).
- **Modular pods.** Terrapin puts modular at about US$50 to US$150 per sq ft more than built-in, but 25 to 40 percent faster. The shell, HVAC interface, exhaust and roof work stay with the general contractor. Compare total installed cost, not the pod quote alone. See [modular vs built-in](/pharmacy-clean-rooms/modular-vs-built-in/).

## How should you budget a clean room project?

**Start from the room program and the NAPRA numbers, get mechanical input before a price, then carry contingency for the air system and certification.**

| Step | What to do |
|---|---|
| 1. Program | List every room, its ISO class, pressure and air changes |
| 2. Site check | Confirm slab, roof, structure, electrical capacity and exhaust routes |
| 3. Early mechanical input | Have the engineer size air handlers and exhaust before the first budget |
| 4. Separate lines | Price classified rooms, support space, HVAC and phasing separately |
| 5. Contingency | Allow for HVAC changes, certification retests and long-lead items |

For a private compounding pharmacy, our [compounding pharmacy design build](/pharmacy-clean-rooms/compounding-pharmacy-design-build/) page explains how one team sets the budget early. For hospital work, see [hospital pharmacy NAPRA upgrade](/pharmacy-clean-rooms/hospital-pharmacy/).

## FAQ

### How much does a NAPRA clean room cost?
It depends on rooms, ISO class and the building. Public BC hospital tenders have been listed from about $350,000 to $6.25M. US sources give US$500 to US$1,200 per sq ft for compounding rooms.

### What is the biggest cost in a pharmacy clean room?
HVAC. Terrapin puts HVAC and filtration at 35 to 55 percent of the clean room budget.

### Is a modular clean room cheaper?
Usually not upfront. One US source puts modular at US$50 to US$150 per sq ft more, but 25 to 40 percent faster to build.

### Why do quotes per square foot vary so much?
Some cover only the ISO rooms and some cover the whole suite. Ask which area a number uses.

### Do you publish your own project prices?
No. Every site and room program differs, so we give a budget after a walk-through and early mechanical input.
