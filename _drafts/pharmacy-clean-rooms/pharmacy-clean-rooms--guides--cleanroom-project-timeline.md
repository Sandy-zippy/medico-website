# How long does it take to build a pharmacy clean room?

*By Dal Riarh, President and CEO, The Medico Group. Last updated 27 November 2026.*

Construction of a pharmacy clean room in a private pharmacy can take about four months, but design, College approval, long-lead equipment and certification add months on either side. A hospital sterile compounding suite takes much longer: public examples run about two to four years from planning to opening, because of procurement and phased work in a pharmacy that stays open.

## How long does a clean room project take overall?

**For a private pharmacy, plan on months, with construction near four months. For a hospital suite, plan on years, mostly planning and procurement.**

| Project | What happened | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Coastal Care Pharmacy, Surrey (our project, 2020) | Contracts signed in May; final electrical and plumbing approvals late August; final building review September. About 16 weeks of construction for a pharmacy with a sterile suite | [Case study](/pharmacy-clean-rooms/projects/coastal-care-sterile-suite/) |
| New BC pharmacy licence | The College estimates 6 to 12 months to open a new pharmacy | [CPBC licensure guide](https://library.bcpharmacists.org/3_Registration_Licensure/5237-Pharmacy-Licensure-Guide.pdf) |
| SHN Scarborough, Ontario (1,200 sq ft hospital clean room) | About two years of planning with "over 15 plan drafts"; work began January 2019; opened July 9, 2020 | [SHN](https://shn.ca/?p=9526) |
| KBRH Trail, BC (pharmacy and ambulatory care) | Construction began 2020; new pharmacy opened July 2022 | [BC Gov News](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2023HLTH0141-001796) |
| Peace Arch Hospital (Fraser Health) | Construction management bid May 2026; construction start listed for January 2027 | [ConstructConnect](https://projects.constructconnect.com/details/7522252-rfp-construction-management-services-for-the-peace-arch-hospital-sterile-compound&find_loc=-V4B-2R4) |

From these examples, a hospital suite takes roughly 12 to 24 months of planning, design and procurement, then 9 to 18 months of phased construction and certification. That is our reading of public projects, not a published benchmark.

## What are the stages of a clean room project?

**Programming, design, College notice, procurement of long-lead items, construction, commissioning, certification, then College evidence before compounding starts in the new rooms.**

| Stage | What happens | Timing notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Program and gap analysis | Rooms, ISO classes, pressures, equipment and workflow | A US architect suggests 5 to 8 weeks for a hospital feasibility study ([LPA](https://lpadesignstudios.com/catalyst/five-strategies-for-upgrading-a-hospital-pharmacy)) |
| 2. Design and permits | Architectural, mechanical, electrical, structural and sprinkler drawings; building permit | Mechanical design drives the schedule |
| 3. College notice (BC) | Form 8G and a scaled diagram | At least 30 days before work starts |
| 4. Long-lead orders | Air handlers, doors, pass-throughs, monitoring, modular pods | Order at design sign-off |
| 5. Construction | Shell, services, rooms, finishes | Phased in hospitals |
| 6. Commissioning | Balancing, controls, pressure monitoring, alarms | Before the certifier arrives |
| 7. Certification | Room and C-PEC testing, air and surface sampling | Allow time for lab results |
| 8. College evidence | Inspection report and photos | Within 14 days of completion |

Sources: [CPBC change in layout](https://www.bcpharmacists.org/node/1758), [CPBC Phase 1 notice](https://www.bcpharmacists.org/node/2450).

## Which items have the longest lead times?

**Air handlers, clean room doors and pass-throughs are the usual long-lead items. One US builder gives about 12 weeks for an AHU.**

JP Cullen, a US contractor, gives about 12 weeks for an air handler and 10 to 12 weeks for FRP doors and pass-throughs ([JP Cullen](https://jpcullen.com/?p=2689)). Lead times vary by supplier and year, so confirm them at design sign-off. Factory-built modular pods are also made to order. On our [Coastal Care compounding lab](/pharmacy-clean-rooms/projects/coastal-care-compounding-lab/), the shell work came first: mezzanine structure for the air handler, rooftop equipment, flooring and a final clean, then the pod install. Read more on [modular vs built-in](/pharmacy-clean-rooms/modular-vs-built-in/) rooms.

## How long do College approvals take?

**In BC, file Form 8G 30 days before work starts, then send evidence within 14 days of finishing. Reviews take two weeks or less.**

The College of Pharmacists of BC asks for PODSA Form 8G and the proposed diagram "no later than 30 days before the start of the renovation," with processing of up to 7 days. After the work, the inspection report and evidence are due "no later than 14 days after the completion date," with processing of up to 14 days, and an on-site inspection may follow ([CPBC](https://www.bcpharmacists.org/node/1758)). In Alberta, the Alberta College of Pharmacy requires approval for pharmacy renovations ([ACP](https://abpharmacy.ca/news/pharmacy-renovations-require-approval-acp/)). Our [pharmacy renovation approval](/pharmacy-clean-rooms/guides/pharmacy-renovation-approval/) guide covers both.

## How long does certification take?

**The certifier's site visit takes hours. Sampling results, any retests and College evidence take longer, so one US builder schedules three to four weeks.**

One certifier's article gives about 1.5 to 2 hours per biological safety cabinet, and says a large suite with several ISO 5 devices "can take well beyond three hours" ([TRC Healthcare](https://trchealthcare.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/How-Long-Should-Cleanroom-Cert-Really-Take_July-2025.pdf)). Air and surface samples then go to a lab. JP Cullen recommends allowing 3 to 4 weeks for certification in the schedule and bringing the certifier in early. Our [pharmacy cleanroom certification](/pharmacy-clean-rooms/certification-commissioning/) page explains what to expect.

## Why do hospital projects take longer?

**Health authorities prequalify firms, run separate design and construction calls, and phase the work so the pharmacy keeps compounding throughout.**

Fraser Health's Abbotsford sterile compounding design RFP was open only to architects "prequalified for Acute Care Projects greater than $150K," with construction listed to start in February 2027 ([ConstructConnect](https://projects.constructconnect.com/details/7522285-rfp-design-sterile-compounding-abbotsford-regional-hospital-and-cancer-centre&find_loc=-V2S-0C2)). Then the phases: UHNBC's NAPRA upgrade had three, starting with demolition of the main compounding area and a temporary non-hazardous compounding room ([ConstructConnect](https://projects.constructconnect.com/details/6474578-university-hospital-of-northern-british-columbia-uhn-pharmacy-napra-upgrades&find_loc=-V2M-1S2)). In our reading of NAPRA, a temporary room is itself a sterile compounding area, so plan for its certification before use. See [hospital pharmacy renovation phasing](/pharmacy-clean-rooms/guides/hospital-pharmacy-phasing/).

## How can you shorten the schedule?

**Lock the room program early, get mechanical design started first, order long-lead items at sign-off, book the certifier early and file College paperwork on time.**

- **Design the exhaust route first.** At Coastal Care Pharmacy, the low-level exhaust route changed several times. Size walls for it in the first drawing issue.
- **Consider modular.** Terrapin says modular rooms build 25 to 40 percent faster than built-in ([Terrapin](https://terrapincg.com/news/cleanroom-construction-cost-per-square-foot-2026)).
- **Use one team.** [Design-build](/guides/design-build-vs-design-bid-build/) lets pricing and design overlap.
- **Commission before certifying.** A failed pressure test means a second certifier visit.

## FAQ

### How long does it take to build a sterile compounding room?
In a private pharmacy, construction can take about four months, as at Coastal Care Pharmacy. Add design, approvals, long-lead equipment and certification.

### How long does a hospital pharmacy clean room take?
Public examples run about two to four years from planning to opening, including procurement and phased construction.

### How much notice does the College of Pharmacists of BC need?
Form 8G is due at least 30 days before the renovation starts, and evidence within 14 days of completion.

### How long does cleanroom certification take?
The site visit takes hours, but plan weeks for lab results and any retests.

### Can we keep compounding during construction?
Hospitals usually phase work and build a temporary compounding room. In our reading of NAPRA, it needs certification before use.
