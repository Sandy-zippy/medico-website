# Z8000 guide: sources (opened 27 Nov 2026)

## Read in full (PDF text extracted)

1. CPSBC, "Facility Design Assessment" policy, Document ID 11731, v1.1, effective 2023-03-25. https://www.cpsbc.ca/files/pdf/NHMSFAP-P-Facility-Design-Assessment.pdf
   - "New non-hospital facilities and existing non-hospital facilities that undergo renovations or change of ownership, as defined by the Bylaws, are required to be in conformance with the current edition of the Canadian Standards Association (CSA) Z8000 Canadian health care facilities standard in effect at the time of the proposed new construction, renovations or change of ownership."
   - "CSA Z8000 Canadian health care facilities is the nationally recognized standard for the planning, design and construction of health care facilities in Canada."
   - Maintain to Z8000, Z8001 (commissioning), Z8002 (operation and maintenance).
   - Tool is provided "when the non-hospital facility confirms that they have obtained their own certified copy of the CSA Z8000 standard."
   - Drawings signed off by the IDT checked "prior to building permitting or construction". Full conformance via "letters of assurance and other supporting documentation from the registered professionals".
2. Fraser Health, Technical Design Requirements, combined sections, Section 01 00 00, version dated December 2025. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/-/media/Project/FraserHealth/FraserHealth/Technical-Design-Requirements/Combined/Technical_Design_Requirements_Combined_Sections.pdf
   - "All Facilities shall be constructed compliant with CSA Z317.13 ... Additionally, all Facilities shall be designed compliant with CSA Z8000 and relevant FHA guidelines. Where available, FH guidelines take precedence."
3. Interior Health, Owner's General Requirements, April 2026. https://www.interiorhealth.ca/sites/default/files/PDFS/owners-general-requirements.pdf
   - "The Contractor must work in compliance with CSA Z317.13-12 "Infection control during construction, renovation, and maintenance of health care facilities" and IH's Infection Prevention and Control Policy."

   - Used for: scope "regardless of type, size, location, or range of services"; new, temporary and renovated facilities; research and testing facilities that perform medical procedures; does not apply to veterinary facilities, funeral homes or mobile health units; does not specify operational models, policies and procedures; 2018 edition = second edition, superseded 2011, added IPC through project stages and the interdisciplinary team's role.

   - Used for: a 2024 edition is listed as most recent.
