Questions owners ask

We already have booking software and a CRM. Why this?

Software is the cheap part. This is the work done for you: content, ads, every enquiry answered, patients followed up, the clinic run.

Do we have to change our booking software?

No. It stays where you book, chart and bill. How the system connects to it is confirmed at your clinic check.

Will the AI say something we haven't approved?

It replies only from answers you approved: prices, hours, services, wording. Anything without an approved answer goes to your desk. Every reply is logged, and you can switch it to drafts-only anytime.

What patient data does it use?

Name, contact details, visit time and recall date. Messages never include a diagnosis, prescription or notes. What patients write in an enquiry stays with that enquiry.

How much of our time does it take?

About three hours of meetings over six weeks, plus a half-day shoot. After launch, about 30 minutes a month to approve content and recalls.

Does it fit our kind of clinic?

Yes: medical, dental, pharmacy, eye care, physio and veterinary.

What happens before we pay?

A free clinic check: your Google, ads, reviews and competitors, with your gaps and numbers. You keep the report either way.

Who do we call when something's off?

Medico. One contact for the room, the marketing and the system.

How is patient privacy handled?

Set up to BC PIPA, or Alberta HIA or PIPA, whichever covers your clinic. A privacy officer is named at setup, with the written policy the law expects, and a privacy lawyer in your province reviews the design before launch. Every login is individual and logged.
